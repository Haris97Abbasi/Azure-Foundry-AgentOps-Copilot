# Incident Policy

## Severity definitions
- SEV1: full outage or risk of customer data loss.
- SEV2: a major feature is degraded with customer impact (for example, checkout errors above 5%).
- SEV3: partial degradation with a workaround available.
- SEV4: minor issue with no immediate customer impact.

## Escalation expectations
- SEV1/SEV2: page the on-call incident commander within 5 minutes; a human must approve any remediation.
- SEV3: the owning team handles it within business hours and escalates if it worsens.
- SEV4: track it in the team backlog.

## Communication rules
- SEV1/SEV2: post a status update in #incidents every 30 minutes.
- Share only confirmed facts and label hypotheses as hypotheses.
- Customer-facing messages go through the support lead.
